How the rules work

  • Closed months (May, Jun): budget = the original FY27 figures, frozen. Re-forecasting later never moves a closed month.
  • Revenue: actual revenue comes straight from the export files; budget revenue = budget units × a per-product/channel price (editable price list, seeded from your budget). Samples carry no revenue.
  • Pro-rata: only offered for the current, in-progress month. Scales that month's budget by days-elapsed ÷ days-in-month so a mid-month export compares fairly.
  • YTD: sums every closed month so far (Apr–Jun now) plus the pro-rata'd current month if you include it.
  • Retail pouches: 1 Upstock carton = 6 units.
One thing I'll confirm during build: the budget-revenue price list (D2C vs retail wholesale price per product). I'll seed it from your budget model and show it for you to sanity-check.
