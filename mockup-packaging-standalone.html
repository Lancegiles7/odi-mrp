Lets items like ecom branded boxes for D2C dispatch exist on their own. Today the form blocks with “Pick a product first.” — every packaging SKU has to be attached to a product.

Same form, same rows. The product picker gains a “no product” option and stops being a blocker.

Qty, Mode and In cost? grey out when no product is picked. All three describe the link between a packaging item and a product — how many go on one unit, and whether that cost lands in the product’s BOM. With no product there’s nothing for them to describe, so today’s values would be silently discarded. Greying them out says so up front.

Button drops “+ link to product” when no product is picked.

3 · Packaging Demand — needs your call

This is the part that isn’t automatic. Demand is calculated as production units × qty per unit — both of which come from a product link.

A standalone ecom box has no product link, so it has no calculated demand. It appears on the Demand page with zeros as soon as it has stock on hand:

A · Leave it manual

Track stock, no forecast. You watch the SOH number and reorder on feel. Nothing more to build.

B · Drive it off D2C orders ← suggested

One box per D2C order. The D2C order forecast already exists, so demand becomes orders × 1. Needs a “consumed per order” field on the item.
